Frequently asked questions

What size is an Indian passport photo?

Indian Passport Seva requires 630 × 810 pixels with a white background.

What size is a US passport photo?

US passport photos are 2 × 2 inches, commonly prepared as 600 × 600 pixels.

Does Passpic upload my photos?

No. All processing happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

Is Passpic free?

Yes. There is no account, no watermark and no charge.
