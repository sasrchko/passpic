OCI Photo Requirements

Requirement Specification
Print Size 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm)
Digital Size 600 × 600 pixels (recommended)
Background Plain white
Colour Colour photograph
Expression Neutral face, mouth closed

Common reasons OCI photos get rejected

  • Wrong size (not exactly 2×2 inches)
  • Background is not white
  • Face is too close or too far from the camera
  • Shadows on the face or background
  • Low resolution or blurry photo
  • Wearing glasses with glare
