Schengen Visa Photo Requirements

Requirement Specification
Size 35 × 45 mm
Background Light-coloured or white
Face Position Head should be between 32–36 mm in height
Expression Neutral, mouth closed
Glasses Generally not recommended

Common reasons Schengen visa photos get rejected

  • Wrong size (not 35×45 mm)
  • Background too dark or with shadows
  • Face too close or too far
  • Smiling or mouth open
  • Wearing glasses with glare
  • Low quality or blurry photo
